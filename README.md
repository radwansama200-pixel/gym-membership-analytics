# Gym Membership & Revenue Analytics Dashboard
Excel dashboard analysing membership, revenue and churn for FitZone Gym (Dakahlia, Egypt), 2025.

**Problem:** The gym owner needed to know which membership plans earn the most and where members are leaving.

**Tools:** Excel (formulas, summary tables, 5 charts, KPI cards)

**What I did:**
- Analysed a 420-member dataset: plan, city, age, trainer, payment method, status
- Calculated KPIs: members, active members, monthly revenue, churn rate, classes per member
- Built summary tables and charts for revenue, city split, monthly signups and churn by plan

**Results:**
- 340 active of 420 members, monthly revenue 156,900 EGP, overall churn 19%
- Churn by plan: Basic 23.4% > Premium 16.4% > VIP 11.3%
- Premium brings the highest revenue (58,500 EGP) even though Basic has more members
- Dekernes is the largest city (160 members, ~38%)

**Recommendation:** Focus on upgrading Basic members to Premium/VIP, since Basic has the highest churn.
